What is Cortex Automation and how does it work?

Updated 2026-11-11 · 4 min read

Cortex Automation is automated trading software: an algorithm that places Bitcoin trades directly in the client's own brokerage account, opened in the client's name. The money never leaves that account, there are no monthly or sign-up fees, and Cortex is only paid a share of the profit, when there is one.

What Cortex Automation is

Cortex Automation is an automated trading platform. Instead of the client opening and closing trades, an algorithm does it automatically, following defined rules and always with the same risk per trade.

The key difference from many investment services is where the money sits: in the client's own brokerage account, opened in their name and with their credentials. Cortex does not hold client funds.

How the system works

The decision engine is based on market structure. Rather than lagging indicators, it reads price action in real time, identifies highs, lows and breakout zones, recalculates levels when new structure appears, and places buys or sells aligned with the trend.

It takes one trade at a time, with predefined risk. It does not use grid or martingale strategies, which can look stable for months and then wipe out an account in a strong trend.

Where the money is and who can access it

Funds stay 100% in the client's personal trading account. The system connects through an API that can only execute trades: it cannot withdraw or transfer money. The client can switch the system off at any time and withdraw directly at the broker, with no lock-up periods or penalties from Cortex.

What it costs

There is no sign-up fee and no monthly fee. Cortex is paid only a percentage of each month's profit. If a month closes without profit, there is nothing to pay. The percentage never applies to the deposited capital.

Results and risks

The monthly track record is published on the website with the negative months included, scaled to 1% risk per trade. Read it carefully: January 2024 to January 2026 are historical-data test results; from February 2026 they come from a real account.

In the published record, the largest drawdown in a single month was 13.83% and the largest peak-to-trough fall was 17.60%. In other words, there are bad months, sometimes in a row. All trading involves significant risk of capital loss, and past performance does not guarantee future results.

How to start

  1. Talk to the Cortex team.
  2. Open an account in your own name with the recommended broker.
  3. Deposit the amount you want to start with.
  4. Connect the system to the account.
  5. Follow everything in real time.

The process takes about 10 minutes.

Who it is for, and who it is not for

It makes sense for people who want exposure to an automated strategy without handing their money to a third party, and who accept there will be negative months. It is not for anyone who needs guaranteed income, or for money needed in the short term. Start with an amount you are comfortable risking.
