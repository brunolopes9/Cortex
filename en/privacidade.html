Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data Cortex Automation collects when you visit this website or contact us, what we use it for, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It has been drafted in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 — the GDPR — and Portuguese Law No. 58/2019.

1. Who is the data controller

The controller of your data is Cortex Automation, which can be contacted on +351 933 938 716 (also via WhatsApp).

Note for the website owner: if the activity is carried out through a legal entity or as a sole trader, replace this paragraph with the full company name, tax identification number (NIF) and registered office address. This is a mandatory disclosure.

2. What data we collect

2.1 Data you provide to us directly

  • First name and surname, when you identify yourself on contacting us.
  • Telephone number and/or WhatsApp identifier, when you send us a message.
  • Email address, if you choose to communicate by that means.
  • Any information you decide to share voluntarily during the conversation (for example, investment objectives or previous experience).

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Technical browsing data: screen width and country. We do not store your IP address — the country comes from the hosting infrastructure, without the IP being transmitted to us.
  • Usage data: sections viewed, time spent on each, how far you scrolled down the page, where the visit came from and which buttons you clicked.
  • A randomly generated number, stored in your browser, solely to distinguish repeat visits by the same person. It does not identify you or track you outside this website.

2.3 Data we do NOT collect

This website does not collect, request or store login credentials for brokerage accounts, bank details, card numbers, passwords or any information that would allow funds to be moved. We will never ask you for such data through this website. If you ever receive a request of this kind in the name of Cortex Automation, treat it as an attempted fraud and contact us on the official number.

3. What we use your data for and on what legal basis

  • Responding to enquiries and requests for information — legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Providing the service and support, should you become a client — legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Measuring and improving use of the website — legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)). The statistics are our own, do not use cookies, do not identify anyone and are not passed on to third parties, which keeps the impact on your privacy to a minimum. You may object at any time using the contact details provided.
  • Complying with legal obligations, in particular tax and accounting obligations — legal basis: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Ensuring the security of the website and preventing misuse — legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)).

We do not use your data for automated decision-making with legal effects, nor for profiling with such an impact.

4. Cookies

We use two categories of cookies:

  • Essential — strictly necessary for the website to function. We store only your cookie choice, in your browser's local storage, so that we do not ask you again. These do not require consent.
  • Statistical — none. The statistics we collect are our own, do not use cookies and do not track anyone across other websites, so they do not depend on the banner. They are described further below.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing the website's data in your browser, which will cause the banner to reappear on your next visit. You can also block or delete cookies directly in your browser settings.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent or transfer your personal data to third parties for commercial purposes. Data may be processed by service providers acting on our behalf and under written instructions, namely:

  • The website hosting provider.
  • The provider of the database in which contacts and visit summaries are stored.
  • An email delivery service, so that we can send you the reports you requested.
  • Messaging platforms you choose to use to contact us (for example, WhatsApp), which process your data in accordance with their own policies, which we recommend you consult.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In such cases, the transfer is based on adequacy decisions of the European Commission or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a contractual relationship: up to 12 months after the last contact.
  • Client data: for the duration of the contractual relationship and, once it ends, for the applicable statutory retention periods, in particular for tax purposes.
  • Statistical data: up to 14 months, in aggregated form.

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or improper disclosure, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS) and restricting access to those who need it. No system is infallible, but we undertake to notify you of any data breach concerning you, in accordance with the legal terms and time limits.

8. Your rights

As a data subject, you have the following rights:

  • Access — to know what data we hold about you and obtain a copy.
  • Rectification — to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — to request the deletion of your data where there are no grounds for retaining it.
  • Restriction — to restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Portability — to receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Objection — to object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdrawal of consent — at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us on +351 933 938 716. We will respond within one month at the latest. If you believe that your data is not being processed correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the national supervisory authority, the Portuguese Data Protection Authority — Comissão Nacional de Protecção de Dados (CNPD).

9. Minors

The services presented on this website are intended exclusively for persons aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware that this has happened, we will delete that data.

10. Links to third-party websites

Data collected when requesting reports

To receive the performance reports, your name and mobile number are requested. Your email is optional.

  • Purpose: to send the reports requested and to answer questions about the service. This use is based on fulfilling the data subject's own request.
  • Marketing communications: monthly results and news are sent only if the authorisation box is ticked. The box is unticked by default, and that authorisation is the sole basis on which we send you campaigns. You may withdraw it at any time, without having to give a reason.
  • Where it is stored: authorised contacts are stored with Resend, the service we use to send email, and a notification of each request is sent to our email. They are not sold, rented or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • How long it is kept: until you withdraw your authorisation or request deletion. If there is no activity for three years, the data is deleted.
  • Your rights: to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object and request your data in a file. Simply ask via WhatsApp on +351 933 938 716 or reply to any of our emails.

Usage statistics

This website's statistics are our own and are kept on our servers. We do not use Google Analytics or any other third-party measurement service: nothing you do here is sent elsewhere.

We collect a summary of each visit: where it came from, how long it lasted, how far you scrolled down the page, which sections you passed through, which buttons you clicked and the screen width. This helps us understand which parts of the website are useful and which need improvement.

To distinguish several visits by the same person from visits by different people, we store a randomly generated number in your browser's local storage. This number neither contains nor reveals anything about you, does not track you across other websites and is not cross-referenced with your name, mobile number or email — visits and contacts are stored separately and are not linked to each other. You can delete it at any time by clearing the website's data in your browser.

We do not store your IP address. The country is obtained through Vercel's infrastructure, without the IP being transmitted to us or retained.

This website contains links to external services, namely WhatsApp and Telegram, used for contact purposes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services and recommend that you read their respective policies.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated whenever justified. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page. Substantial changes will be communicated visibly on the website.

This document is a base template, adapted to the service described on this website. Before putting the website into production, have it validated by a lawyer or data protection officer, particularly as regards the identification of the responsible entity and the regulatory framework of the activity.
