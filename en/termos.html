5. Remuneration model

The service has no joining fee or monthly fees. Remuneration is based exclusively on a percentage of the profits actually generated, according to the tier applicable to the capital deposited:

  • Standard — capital from €500 to €999: 60% to the client, 40% to Cortex Automation.
  • Pro — capital from €1,000 to €9,999: 75% to the client, 25% to Cortex Automation.
  • VIP — capital from €10,000: 80% to the client, 20% to Cortex Automation.

5.1 Calculation rule

Cortex Automation's remuneration is calculated monthly and applies exclusively to the profit generated in the month, understood as the positive net result of the trades closed in that period.

  • If the month closes with a profit, the percentage of the contracted tier is applied to that profit. Example: in a month with 10% profit, on the Pro tier, Cortex Automation receives 25% of that profit and the client keeps the remaining 75%.
  • If the month closes without a profit or with a negative result, no amount is owed by the client. There are no minimum amounts, monthly fees, penalties or amounts carried over as debt to subsequent months.
  • The percentage never applies to the capital deposited, only to profit.
  • Each month is calculated independently: the result of one month is not offset against that of previous months for the purpose of calculating remuneration.
  • Deposits and withdrawals made during the month do not change the calculation basis, which is the result of the trades closed in the period.
  • Costs charged by the broker — namely spreads, commissions, swaps and any transaction fees — are separate from this remuneration and the responsibility of the client, under the terms of the contract entered into with the broker.

The specific payment conditions and frequency are communicated to the client in writing before joining.
