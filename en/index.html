How profit sharing is calculated

  • It applies only to the month’s profit. Your tier’s percentage is applied to the profit generated in that month — never to the capital you deposited.
  • No profit in a month, nothing to pay. If the month closes without profit, absolutely nothing is charged. No minimums, no monthly fee and nothing left owing.
  • Each month is assessed on its own. The calculation is made month by month, based on that month’s result.
